The engraving mark

An orange diamond means that hero is further enhanced with Engraving. Lupico is the example of an energy battery, and Dmitri of a damage cap. Stars are a separate question: Roze shines at 18 stars, while LuLu and Solina already work at low stars.

A glance, not a team

These recommendations can differ from other playstyles. Synergy matters, and a lower rank can still be the right pick for a specific fight. One pairing from the guide: Rakan’s silence lets Carmen and Benzel do their damage.

Understanding synergy

Placement

Placement is key. A solid roster still falls short if the composition has no synergy, and the heroes never get used fully. Common examples:

Richelle

He needs to stand next to allies so Damage Cap covers them, which lets the comp last much further into Journey. He usually sits in the second row, with a hero in front to sustain and tank, such as Carmen, Calix, Nuel, or Rakan.

Lilith

A strong dealer into several frontliners. Against multiple enemies she deals big damage, drains their energy, and keeps going. She also lines up with Zagrako and Sander for some obstacles in Journey.

Heroes

Not every hero works if you drop them in at random. Some low-star heroes, and even low-tier ones, do very well in a specific case.

Sacrificial heroes

Rakan, Lagou, Zagrako, Ash, and Luke. Their kit lets a debuff or a buff land, and that is what the team gains from them.

Bakra

Even at low stars, Bakra holds several attacks through his Strengthen passive.

Utility heroes

Galan, Idina, and Olga. Useful for a given stage. Which one you bring changes with the chapter, and with engraving.

There are many cases where these factors decide the best result.
